Categories

Opening this screen starts with all categories selected. Choose “Choose Categories” to clear the list, then tick only the categories you want for your next game.

Rules

Multiplayer

  1. A category is chosen at random. Press START, then wait for 3, 2, 1, GO!
  2. Say an answer that fits the category, then tap the letter your answer starts with.
  3. Press the middle buzzer to reset the timer for the next player.
  4. A used letter cannot be used again until every letter A–Z has been used.
  5. When all 26 letters are used, press the middle buzzer as normal and A–Z refreshes for the next player.
  6. The round ends only when somebody runs out of time.

Solo

  1. Choose Solo on the main menu, then start the round.
  2. Type an answer and press Enter or Submit.
  3. Your answer must begin with an unused A–Z letter.
  4. A valid answer marks that letter as used and immediately resets the timer.
  5. After all 26 letters are used, the alphabet automatically refreshes and you keep going.
  6. Tappity does not verify whether the answer actually fits the category, so play on the honour system.
  7. Your answer count is your score. Your best score is saved on this browser/device.
