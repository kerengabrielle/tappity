A quick-fire category game

Categories

Opening this screen starts with all categories selected. Choose “Choose Categories” to clear the list, then select only the categories you want to use.

Rules

Multiplayer

  1. A category is chosen at random. Press START and wait for 3, 2, 1, GO!
  2. Say an answer that fits the category.
  3. Tap the letter your answer begins with.
  4. Press the centre buzzer to reset the timer and pass play to the next person.
  5. Used letters cannot be selected again during the current alphabet cycle.
  6. Once all 26 letters have been used, press the buzzer as normal. A–Z will become available again for the next player.
  7. The round ends only when somebody runs out of time.

Solo

  1. Choose Solo on the main menu and start the round.
  2. When the timer begins, first tap an available letter on the board.
  3. The selected letter turns yellow and the answer box appears.
  4. You may change your selected letter by tapping another available letter before submitting your answer.
  5. Type an answer beginning with the selected letter.
  6. Press Enter or Submit. The answer will only be accepted if it begins with your selected letter.
  7. After a successful answer, that letter becomes used, your answer count increases, the answer box disappears, and the timer resets.
  8. Select another available letter and repeat.
  9. The timer continues running while you choose a letter and type your answer. Selecting a letter does not pause or reset the clock.
  10. Once all 26 letters have been used, A–Z automatically becomes available again.
  11. Tappity checks only whether your answer starts with the selected letter. It does not verify whether the answer genuinely fits the category, so play on the honour system.
  12. Your number of successful answers is your score. Your best Solo score is saved on the current browser/device.
